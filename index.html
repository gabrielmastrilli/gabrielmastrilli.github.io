About

I am a PhD candidate in statistics at ENSAI, Rennes, with an affiliation at INRIA, Paris. My thesis is supervised by Frédéric Lavancier at ENSAI and Bartłomiej Błaszczyszyn at INRIA. My PhD defense is scheduled for July 2026.

My PhD focuses on second-order inference for stationary spatial point processes. I study Fourier-domain summaries, such as the structure factor, which play a role analogous to spectral densities in time series analysis.

Research interests

  • Spatial statistics and point processes
  • Spectral estimation
  • Hyperuniformity
